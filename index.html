Danielbot

A personal software project maintained by the owner of the GitHub account kokoetebassey.

Project purpose

Danielbot is being developed to test a multi-timeframe trading strategy on the developer's own cTrader demo accounts, using cTrader Open API and Railway hosting.

Testing includes reading market quotes and candle data, evaluating strategy conditions, and verifying risk controls before any demo orders are enabled.

Current status

The project is under development for analysis-only and demo testing. It is not currently offered as a trading service to other users and does not accept customer funds.

Account access and privacy

Account connections require explicit authorization through cTrader. This public information page does not request trading passwords, access tokens, or deposits.

Risk notice

This project does not provide investment advice or guarantee returns. Demo results do not establish future live trading performance. Trading can result in losses.
